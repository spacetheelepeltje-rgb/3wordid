Retrograde Satellite at GEO Altitude (42,164 km)

Blue dot = Retrograde Satellite (very fast) | Green dot = Normal Prograde GEO | Yellow = Sun direction

Notice how the retrograde satellite (blue) whips around the Earth extremely fast in the opposite direction, while the normal GEO satellite (green) stays nearly fixed above one spot.

Even at this high speed, the retrograde satellite still spends half its time on the night side.
